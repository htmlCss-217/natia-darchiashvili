About Trello

Trello, is the travel guidance platform, helps people to become better travelers, from planning to booking to taking a trip. Travelers across the globe use Trello's website and app to discover where to stay, what to do and where to eat based on guidance from those who have been there before. With more than a billion reviews and contributions, travelers turn to Tripadvisor to find deals on accommodations, book experiences, reserve tables at delicious restaurants and discover great places nearby.
