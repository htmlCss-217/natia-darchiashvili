Temperature Converter

Temperature degrees can be converted in Fahrenheit (°F), Celsius (°C), Kelvin (K), Rankine (°R).



Celsius °C to Fahrenheit °F Formula:

°F equals °C multiplied by 9/5, plus 32. °F = °C * 9/5 + 32

Fahrenheit is a temperature scale and degrees Fahrenheit are units of temperature on that scale.

Celsius is a temperature scale and degrees Celsius are units of temperature on that scale.
